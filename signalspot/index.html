Signal Spot – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026 · Deutsche Version

Signal Spot is an app that measures the mobile signal (4G/5G) of your phone to help you find the best place in your home for a mobile router. It is built so that no data leaves your device.

Who is responsible

Signal Spot is developed by Mohammed Al Balkhi (the "controller" under the EU General Data Protection Regulation, GDPR).
Email: pdfchanger73@gmail.com

Summary

  • We do not collect any personal data. No accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking.
  • The app has no internet access. The Android app does not even have permission to use the internet, so nothing can be sent anywhere.
  • Your location is never used or stored.

Location permission

Android only shares details of the mobile signal (signal strength, quality, frequency band and the ID of the cell your phone is connected to) with apps that have location permission, and only while location is switched on. Signal Spot asks for this permission only to read these signal values. The app does not request, use or store your GPS position, and the signal values are processed only on your device.

Information stored on your device

When you measure a place, the app saves the name you give it (for example "Kitchen") and the measured signal values, so you can compare places. This is stored only on your device, in the app's private storage, and is not included in cloud backups. You can delete single places or all places in the app at any time. Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to information about, correction of, deletion of and restriction of processing of your personal data, the right to data portability and the right to object. Because the app collects no personal data, we usually hold none about you. To use your rights, email us. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority.
